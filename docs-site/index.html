01 · Getting started

Start with the dashboard.

Sign in

Use the Auctrail account provided for your organization. Contact your administrator when you need an account, a different role, or access to a department.

Review current work

The Dashboard brings together case totals, review and auction-stage counts, priority work, and intake activity.

Open the relevant workspace

Use the navigation to reach Assets, Cases, Intake, Disposition, Reports, or Administration when those areas are available to your role.

02 · Assets & intake

Create the appropriate record.

Asset tracking and surplus intake serve different purposes. Use the option that matches the property and your organization’s operating mode.

1

Choose single or bulk intake

Open Intake. Received Property creates surplus property records. Received Assets adds asset-only records when asset tracking is enabled. Select Single for one record or Bulk for a supported spreadsheet import.

2

Enter and review the information

Check identifiers, descriptions, department, condition, quantity, and other applicable details before saving or completing the import.

3

Check imported batches

Open Import History from Administration to review batch details and the resulting cases. Use the available search and department or uploader filters to locate a batch.

03 · Cases & closeout

Keep active cases and completed outcomes organized.

Find the case

Open Cases and use search or the status, department, and photo filters. Open the relevant row to review the case. Available row actions depend on permissions.

Prepare the record

Review the description, approvals, disposition method, photos, documents, and required supporting information before moving ahead.

Record the result

Keep sale results, payment, pickup, dates, proof, and closeout notes with the case as required by your organization.

Return to completed outcomes

Use Disposition to locate completed Sold, Destroyed, and Transferred outcomes. Apply the outcome, search, and date filters, then select Open Case to view a record.

04 · Photos & documents

Prepare auction media without disconnecting it from the case.

1

Upload files

Open a case and find the photo/document upload area. Drop files into the upload area or click to browse. Supported formats are JPG, PNG, WebP, and PDF, with a 15 MB limit per file and up to 15 files per selection.

2

Order the photos

Arrange uploaded photos in the sequence needed for the auction listing. Confirm the order has saved before exporting.

3

Download Images

Click Download Images on the right side of the upload area. Auctrail prepares the case photos in a ZIP using saved order and sequential JPG filenames. Wait for preparation to finish and open the downloaded ZIP before uploading to the auction site.

4

Keep supporting documents with the record

PDFs remain attached as documents and are not included in the photo ZIP. The download action is unavailable when the case has no uploaded photos.

05 · Reports & exports

Review the information already in Auctrail.

Set the scope

Open Reports and use the available search, department, status, disposal, condition, and date filters to narrow the records.

Review totals

Check the filtered records, open records, sold items, and sales totals before using them for management or reconciliation work.

Export available data

Use the available Excel or CSV export controls for the workflow you are reviewing. Confirm the record scope and selected filters before sharing the file.

06 · Administration

Configure people, workflow, and appearance.

People and organization

Manage users, assigned roles, departments, and organization settings. Grant only the permissions required for each person’s responsibilities.

Operating mode

Review whether your organization uses Asset Tracking + Surplus or Surplus Only before setting up routine intake processes.

Branding

Open Branding to review the organization name, supported color schemes, and organization icon. Use the live preview and select Save Appearance to apply changes.

Pickup deadline

Open Pickup Deadline and set the calendar-day period used for sold-item pickup. The screen explains the default, allowed range, and how the timer begins and stops. Save the deadline after making a change.

07 · Labels & QR access

Keep the case identifier with the item.

A case label includes the identifier, property description, and QR code. Authorized staff can scan the code to open the associated record. Authentication or organization access may be required.

Need help?

Use Auctrail Support for account access or technical issues. General product, availability, and pricing questions can be sent to info@auctrai.com.
