01 · Getting started

Start with your organization and the people doing the work.

Sign in

Use your Auctrail account to access the hosted application. Your available navigation and actions depend on your assigned role and organization permissions.

Organization structure

Administrators can define departments, locations, users, and operational settings so records are assigned to the correct team and responsibility remains visible.

Navigation

The web application centers work around assets, surplus cases, disposition activity, reports, administration, and support. Use search and case identifiers to return to existing records quickly.

02 · Assets & surplus property

Create one durable record before property moves through disposition.

1

Create or import the record

Capture the property identifier, department, location, description, condition, quantity, custody details, and notes. Import tools may be used when an organization is moving existing records into Auctrail.

2

Review the information

Confirm the record is complete enough for the organization’s approval and disposition process. Correct missing or inaccurate information before the property moves forward.

3

Move to surplus/disposition

Once property is approved for disposition, use the case workflow to prepare it for sale, transfer, donation, destruction, or the organization’s approved disposition method.

03 · Cases & workflow

Keep status, responsibility, and next action visible.

Review

Use the case record to verify approvals, ownership, disposition method, assignments, and supporting information.

Prepare

Add descriptions, photos, documents, labels, pricing, and auction details. Resolve any missing requirements before listing or final disposition.

Sell / dispose

Record the selected sale source or disposition method and the outcome when the property leaves active preparation.

Payment & pickup

For sold property, preserve buyer information, amount, payment details, pickup status, dates, and proof required by your organization.

Closeout

Complete the record only after the required result, proof, notes, and disposition details have been captured.

History

Completed records remain available for search, reporting, audit review, and records-management needs.

04 · Photos & documents

Keep auction media and supporting files with the case.

Upload files

Use the case photo/document area to drag and drop or browse for JPG, PNG, WebP, and PDF files. The current uploader supports up to 15 files at a time and a 15 MB limit per file.

Order auction photos

Drag uploaded photos into the sequence you want to use for the auction listing. Auctrail saves the order so the exported image set follows the same sequence.

Download Images

Use Download Images beside the upload area to prepare all uploaded case photos as a ZIP file. Images are exported in saved order as sequential JPG files for easier use on auction sites.

Documents

PDF documents are stored separately from auction photos so approvals, titles, receipts, and other supporting records stay attached without being included in the photo ZIP.

05 · Reports & exports

Reuse the information already maintained in Auctrail.

Operational reports

Use reports to review active work, disposition results, completed records, and other management information available to your role.

CSV / Excel output

Export structured data when administration, finance, reconciliation, or records-retention workflows require a file outside Auctrail.

Auction preparation

Auctrail is being developed to reduce duplicate listing work by reusing approved case information and media for supported auction workflows and exports.

06 · Administration

Configure the organization without losing control of access.

Users & roles

Administrators manage user access and roles. Users should receive only the permissions required for their responsibilities.

Departments & locations

Maintain the organizational structure used to assign records, filter work, report activity, and identify responsibility.

Branding & settings

Available organization settings can be used to tailor the hosted workspace while keeping the underlying Auctrail workflow consistent.

Support

For account-specific questions or technical issues, use Auctrail Support so the request can be tracked and responded to with the correct context.

Need help?

Open Auctrail Support for account-specific questions or technical issues. General product and pricing questions can be sent to info@auctrai.com.
