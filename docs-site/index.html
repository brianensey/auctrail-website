01 · Getting started

Start with your organization and the people doing the work.

Sign in

Use your Auctrail account to access the hosted application. Your available navigation and actions depend on your assigned role and organization permissions.

Organization structure

Administrators can define departments, locations, users, and operational settings so records are assigned to the correct team and responsibility remains visible.

Navigation

The web application centers work around assets, cases, intake, disposition, reports, administration, and support.

02 · Assets & surplus property

Create one durable record before property moves through disposition.

1

Create or import the record

Capture the property identifier, department, location, description, condition, quantity, custody details, and notes.

2

Review the information

Confirm the record is complete enough for the organization’s approval and disposition process.

3

Move to surplus/disposition

Once approved, move the property into the applicable case and disposition workflow.

03 · Cases & workflow

Keep status, responsibility, and next action visible.

Review

Use the case record to verify approvals, ownership, disposition method, assignments, and supporting information.

Prepare

Add descriptions, photos, documents, labels, pricing, and auction details.

Sell / dispose

Record the sale source or disposition method and the outcome.

Payment & pickup

For sold property, preserve buyer information, amount, payment details, pickup status, dates, and proof.

Closeout

Complete the record after required proof, notes, and disposition details have been captured.

History

Completed records remain available for search, reporting, audit review, and records-management needs.

04 · Photos & documents

Keep auction media and supporting files with the case.

The approved screenshot set does not currently include the photo/document manager, so this section intentionally does not show an unrelated image.

Upload files

Use the case photo/document area to drag and drop or browse for JPG, PNG, WebP, and PDF files. The current uploader supports up to 15 files at a time and a 15 MB limit per file.

Order auction photos

Drag uploaded photos into the sequence you want to use for the auction listing.

Download Images

Use Download Images beside the upload area to prepare uploaded case photos as a ZIP file in saved order.

Documents

PDF documents remain attached to the case without being included in the auction-photo ZIP.
